# SponsorFit Report Template

Use this order. Keep the report skimmable and specific.

1. **Project / Idea Snapshot** — what it does, users, value, maturity or assumptions, readiness
2. **What This Project Really Sells** — 3–7 purchased outcomes
3. **Competitive Landscape** — direct, indirect, commercial analogs, and status quo; explain why each is comparable
4. **How Competitors Make Money** — one evidence-labeled dossier per deep competitor covering buyer, offer, price, sales motion, customer evidence, financial status, and inferred business model
5. **Competitor Commercial Comparison** — entry offer, acquisition, conversion event, pricing unit, retention, expansion, and evidence strength
6. **Ideal Customer Ranking** — evidence, score, budget, required features
7. **Your Happiest Sponsors** — first-person simulations and one winner
8. **Ranked Monetization Paths** — 3–5 paths with analog evidence, fit, burden, and falsification test
9. **Recommended Business Model** — one primary path, at most one secondary experiment, and why it can win
10. **What Stays Free / What Gets Paid / Never Paywall**
11. **Pricing and Sales Hypothesis** — range, unit, sales motion, and validation method
12. **How to Make the First $1,000** — target, channel, offer, price, message, test
13. **Revenue Ladder** — first $100, $1,000, and $10,000
14. **Top Things to Build Next** — at least five across Revenue, Adoption, Trust, Enterprise, Community
15. **Risks and Unknowns** — demand, financial evidence, license, community, capacity, competition
16. **7-Day Action Plan** — one observable commercial action per day

End with:

```text
YOUR BEST CUSTOMER:
...

THEY PAY FOR:
...

BEST BUSINESS MODEL:
...

WHY THIS CAN WIN:
...

BUILD THIS NEXT:
...

FIRST REVENUE MOVE:
...
```

## Evidence language

- Prefix direct facts with **Observed** and cite repository paths or GitHub surfaces.
- Prefix reasoned conclusions with **Inferred** and show the facts used.
- Prefix buyer, demand, price, and channel claims with **Hypothesis** until externally validated.
- Prefer “No evidence found” over filling a blank with a generic claim.
